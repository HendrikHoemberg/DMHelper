# Campaign Preparation

## Adventures & Scenes

Organise your campaign into adventures, chapters, and scenes. Each scene can hold body text (scene notes), links to maps, encounters, and handouts, and typed transitions (CHOICE/ENTRANCE/EXIT) to other scenes.

## Maps

Upload map images via the Maps section. Published maps support:
- Calibrate, crop, rotate, and lock
- Named regions with keys for scene/encounter placement
- DM/player layer split (shared tokens with `playerVisible` flags)

## Encounters

Create encounters from the Encounters list or directly from the library. Each encounter tracks combatants, initiative, HP, conditions, and round count. Difficulty estimates use 2014 DMG thresholds and are labeled **Estimate** (2024 authoritative estimates are partial — see [Campaign Capabilities](../campaign-capabilities.md)).

## Party Sheets

Add party members in the Party section. Each member tracks ability scores, class levels, proficiencies, resources, spells, attacks, features, inventory, and live combat state (HP, temp HP, death saves, conditions).

## Notes & Handouts

Use typed notes with wiki links and backlinks for campaign documentation. Quick notes provide a fast-capture surface. Handouts can be marked DM-only or player-safe.

## Calendar

Set up a custom calendar with named months, weekdays, and events. The in-game date is displayed in the session cockpit and can be advanced during play.
